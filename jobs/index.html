Jobs

We’re looking for experienced systems programmers

We are interested in hearing from skilled systems programmers with a strong command of computer architecture, operating systems, compilers, hypervisors, and/or trusted execution environments. Language wise, strong command of Rust, C/C++, x86 or arm assembly plus attendant build systems and tools is a plus.

If you think you’re a good fit, please send us an email at team@immunant.com or use our contact form. We’d like to hear how your previous experience lines up with the work we’ve published on GitHub. Similarly, we’d love to see your code if there’s a project or project contribution you’d like to share. Candidates without relevant skills or experience and tech recruiters are not encouraged to write.

We cannot consider applicants without authorization to work in the United States or applicants residing outside of the United States. Immunant uses e-Verify to verify work authorization. The base salary for software engineering positions ranges from $110K to $150K based on experience and skill set.
